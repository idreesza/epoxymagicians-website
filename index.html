Epoxy flooring FAQs

How much does garage floor epoxy cost in DFW?

Most 2-car garage floors in Dallas–Fort Worth run $1,800–$4,500 installed. Flake systems typically start near $5–$8 per square foot and metallic systems near $8–$12 per square foot, depending on concrete repair and coating system.

How long does epoxy last in Texas heat?

A professionally installed polyaspartic or epoxy floor lasts 15–20+ years. Polyaspartic topcoats are UV-stable, so they resist the yellowing and softening that summer heat causes in cheap DIY kits.

How soon can I park on it?

With a polyaspartic system you can walk on it in a few hours and park in about 24 hours. Traditional epoxy needs 24–72 hours before vehicle traffic.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes. Once fully cured, these coatings are inert, non-toxic and easy to clean. We use low-VOC products and ventilate during install.
