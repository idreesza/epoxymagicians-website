Flake (Chip) Epoxy Flooring in Dallas–Fort Worth

The most popular garage floor in DFW — durable, slip-resistant, and forgiving of the minor slab imperfections common in North Texas homes.

Flake epoxy (also called chip epoxy) broadcasts colored vinyl chips into a wet epoxy base coat to full or partial coverage, then seals it with a clear topcoat. It's the flooring system we install most often across Dallas–Fort Worth because it balances durability, slip resistance, and price better than any other coating — and it hides minor slab flaws that would show through a solid-color floor.

Flake / Chip Epoxy cost in DFW: $5–$8/sq ft installed. A typical 2-car garage (400–500 sq ft) runs $2,000–$4,000 installed, including grinding, crack repair and topcoat.

Why flake is the default choice for DFW garages

Most residential slabs in North Texas have some combination of hairline cracks, patch repairs, or surface mottling from age and our region's clay soil movement. A flake floor's broken color pattern camouflages these imperfections far better than a solid or metallic finish. Flake also adds real texture underfoot for slip resistance — important for a space that gets wet from cars, hoses, and Texas thunderstorms tracked in on tires.

How we install a flake epoxy floor

Every flake floor we install follows the same disciplined prep sequence:

  1. Diamond grinding. We mechanically grind the full slab — never acid-etch — to open the concrete's pores for a mechanical bond.
  2. Crack & joint repair. Cracks and pitting are filled with a structural polymer and ground flush.
  3. Epoxy base coat. A high-solids epoxy base is rolled to full coverage and thickness.
  4. Flake broadcast. Vinyl flake is broadcast by hand to full refusal (or a lighter accent broadcast, if you prefer) in your chosen color blend.
  5. Scrape & vacuum. Once cured, loose flake is scraped and vacuumed to a smooth, even texture.
  6. Polyaspartic topcoat. A UV-stable clear topcoat locks the flake in place and sets the final sheen and slip resistance.

Flake / Chip Epoxy FAQs

How much does flake epoxy cost in DFW?

Flake epoxy runs $5–$8 per square foot installed. A 2-car garage typically costs $2,000–$4,000 depending on slab prep needed.

Is flake epoxy slip-resistant?

Yes — the broadcast flake creates real surface texture, and we can add an extra anti-slip additive to the topcoat for wet areas like patios or pool decks.

Can I pick a custom flake color blend?

Yes. We carry standard blends and can special-order others to match a specific look. We'll bring physical samples to your estimate.

How does flake compare to metallic epoxy?

Flake is more durable-feeling underfoot, more slab-flaw-forgiving, and less expensive. Metallic is higher gloss and more visually dramatic. See our metallic epoxy page for a full comparison.
