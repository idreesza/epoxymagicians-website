A textured, UV-stable coating that stays cooler underfoot than bare concrete — built for chlorine, sun and bare feet.

Patio and pool deck coating is a textured, UV-stable polyaspartic system designed for outdoor concrete that takes constant sun, chlorine and bare feet. Bare concrete around a DFW pool gets hot enough by mid-afternoon in July to burn bare feet; our textured coatings reduce surface temperature and add slip resistance while covering stains, cracks and discoloration in the existing slab.

Patio & Pool Deck Coating cost in DFW: $6–$10/sq ft installed. Pricing depends on square footage and texture level; most residential patios and pool decks run $2,500–$7,000 installed.

Built for Texas sun and chlorine

Outdoor concrete has different demands than a garage floor: constant UV exposure, chlorine and pool chemicals, temperature swings, and bare feet that need slip resistance instead of gloss. We use polyaspartic systems almost exclusively outdoors because standard epoxy will amber and can become brittle in direct Texas sun within a couple of seasons.

How we coat a patio or pool deck

Outdoor slabs get the same rigorous prep as our interior floors, plus weather-specific steps:

  1. Diamond grinding. The slab is ground to remove old sealer, stains and laitance and open the surface for bonding.
  2. Crack & spall repair. Common on older DFW patios from years of soil movement and sun exposure.
  3. Polyaspartic base coat. Applied with a texture additive built in for slip resistance around water.
  4. Color & texture pass. Decorative color flake or a knockdown texture can be added for a stone-like look.
  5. UV-stable topcoat. Seals everything and provides the cooling, non-slip surface finish.

Does it actually stay cooler?

Yes — the textured, lighter-colored finishes we use reflect more heat than dark bare concrete, which is a major factor in surface temperature around a pool in July and August. We can walk you through color options at your estimate with heat performance in mind.

Patio & Pool Deck Coating FAQs

Does pool deck coating get hot in Texas summer?

Our textured, UV-stable coatings run noticeably cooler than bare concrete because of their color and texture, though lighter colors perform best in direct sun.

Is it resistant to pool chemicals?

Yes, polyaspartic systems resist chlorine and common pool chemicals without discoloring.

How long before I can walk on it?

Typically a few hours for foot traffic once the topcoat is applied, given polyaspartic's fast cure.

Can it cover existing stains or cracks?

Yes — we repair cracks and the coating fully covers staining and discoloration in the existing slab.
