Commercial and industrial epoxy flooring is a heavy-duty, seamless coating system engineered for forklift traffic, chemical exposure and constant foot traffic in warehouses, manufacturing plants, restaurants and retail spaces. Epoxy Magicians works with DFW business owners and facility managers to schedule installs around operating hours — nights, weekends, or phased sections — so you don't lose a full day of business.

Commercial & Industrial Epoxy cost in DFW: Commercial pricing depends on square footage, traffic load, chemical exposure and downtime constraints — every job gets a written, itemized quote.

Built for how your business actually uses the floor

A commercial floor takes abuse a residential garage never sees: pallet jacks and forklifts, chemical spills, constant foot traffic, and sometimes 24/7 operation. We spec the system to the use case — high-build epoxy or polyaspartic for heavy traffic, chemical-resistant novolac epoxy for manufacturing and auto shops, anti-static (ESD) flooring for electronics or server environments, and safety-yellow striping or floor markings where OSHA compliance matters.

How we minimize your downtime

We know a closed floor is lost revenue, so commercial jobs are planned around your business, not ours:

  1. Walkthrough & spec. We assess traffic type, chemical exposure and any code requirements (striping, drainage slope, ESD) before quoting.
  2. Phased or off-hours scheduling. Large facilities are often coated in sections, or work happens nights/weekends so you stay open.
  3. Diamond grinding. Industrial-grade grinders prep the slab for full-strength adhesion under heavy loads.
  4. High-build system application. Base coats are applied thicker than residential systems to withstand wheeled and foot traffic.
  5. Fast-cure topcoat. We use polyaspartic or fast-cure epoxy topcoats to get you back on the floor as quickly as possible.

Commercial & Industrial Epoxy FAQs

How much does commercial epoxy flooring cost?

Commercial pricing varies widely by square footage, traffic type and required chemical resistance, so we provide a written quote after a walkthrough rather than a flat per-square-foot number.

Can you install without closing our business?

In most cases, yes — we regularly schedule commercial jobs at night, on weekends, or in phased sections so operations continue.

Is your commercial epoxy rated for forklift traffic?

Yes, we spec high-build epoxy or polyaspartic systems designed for forklift and pallet-jack traffic based on your facility's load.

Do you offer safety striping or floor markings?

Yes, we can add safety-yellow aisle striping, ADA markings, or custom floor graphics as part of the install.
