Driveway Coating in Dallas–Fort Worth

A UV-stable polyaspartic system built for the toughest concrete on your property — full sun, daily tire traffic, and Texas temperature swings.

Driveway coating applies a UV-stable polyaspartic system directly to your existing concrete driveway, resisting hot-tire pickup, sun fading and cracking far better than sealer or paint. A driveway is the harshest environment we coat — full sun all day, hot tires, de-icing exposure in rare winter events, and constant weight — so we build every driveway system with polyaspartic rather than standard epoxy.

Driveway Coating cost in DFW: Driveways vary widely in size and slab condition, so we provide a written quote after measuring — most residential driveways run $3,000–$9,000 installed.

Why driveways need a different system than garages

Standard epoxy will amber and can soften enough for a parked car's hot tires to pull the coating loose — a failure called hot-tire pickup. Because polyaspartic is UV-stable and has better heat resistance, it's the only system we recommend for full-sun driveway applications. It also cures fast enough that your driveway isn't unusable for days.

How we coat a driveway

Driveway prep accounts for the extra wear and larger surface area:

  1. Diamond grinding. The full driveway is mechanically ground to remove old sealer and open the surface for bonding.
  2. Crack & expansion joint repair. Driveway cracks from soil movement are filled and reinforced; expansion joints are either honored or filled per your preference.
  3. Polyaspartic base coat. Applied at a heavier build than interior floors to handle vehicle weight and turning stress.
  4. Decorative flake (optional). Color flake can be added for a textured, stone-like look that also hides tire marks.
  5. UV-stable topcoat. Locks everything in and provides the final slip-resistant, sun-stable finish.

What about hot-tire pickup?

Hot-tire pickup happens when a coating softens in heat and a parked tire's traction pulls it loose. It's almost always a sign of standard epoxy used outdoors, or poor slab prep. Our polyaspartic driveway systems and diamond-ground prep are specifically chosen to prevent it.

Driveway Coating FAQs

Will a driveway coating fade in the sun?

Not with our polyaspartic systems — they're UV-stable and formulated for constant sun exposure, unlike standard epoxy.

What is hot-tire pickup and will it happen to my driveway?

It's when a coating softens under a hot, parked tire and lifts. Proper prep and a polyaspartic system (not standard epoxy) prevent it.

How long does a driveway coating take to install?

Most residential driveways are completed in one to two days depending on size, with vehicle traffic possible around 24 hours after the final coat.

How much does a driveway coating cost?

It depends heavily on square footage and slab condition, so we provide a written quote after measuring — most residential driveways run $3,000–$9,000.
