Concrete Polishing in Dallas–Fort Worth

No topcoat to wear through — just your slab, mechanically ground and polished to a durable satin or high-gloss sheen.

Concrete polishing mechanically grinds and densifies the existing slab through progressively finer diamond tooling until it reaches a durable satin or high-gloss sheen — with no coating applied on top. It's a different approach from epoxy: instead of adding a topcoat, we're refining the concrete itself, which means there's no film to wear through, chip or peel.

Concrete Polishing cost in DFW: $4–$8/sq ft installed. Pricing depends on the polish level (matte, satin or high-gloss) and whether dye or stain is added.

Polished concrete vs. epoxy — which is right for you?

Polished concrete is the lowest-maintenance flooring option we offer and holds up indefinitely to foot and light vehicle traffic, which is why it's popular in warehouses, retail showrooms, and homeowners who want an industrial-modern look. It doesn't hide slab imperfections the way flake epoxy does, and it won't have the vivid color options of metallic epoxy — but it also never needs a recoat.

How the polishing process works

Polishing is a dustless, multi-pass mechanical process:

  1. Initial grind. Coarse diamond tooling removes surface contamination and exposes the aggregate level you want (cream, salt-and-pepper, or full aggregate).
  2. Densifier application. A chemical densifier hardens the slab's surface and reduces future dusting.
  3. Progressive fine grinding. We step through progressively finer diamond grits, refining the surface toward your target sheen.
  4. Optional dye or stain. Concrete dye can be added before final polishing for color.
  5. Guard/sealer. A penetrating guard is applied for stain resistance without changing the polished look.

Sheen levels

We offer everything from a matte, low-sheen finish (common in warehouses) to a full high-gloss mirror finish (popular in retail and residential great rooms). All-dust is captured during grinding with vacuum-shrouded equipment — the process is far cleaner than most homeowners expect.

Concrete Polishing FAQs

How is polished concrete different from epoxy?

Polished concrete refines the slab itself with no topcoat, so there's nothing to chip or peel, but it doesn't hide slab imperfections or offer the vivid color/pattern options of epoxy.

How much does concrete polishing cost?

Typically $4–$8 per square foot depending on the sheen level and whether dye is added.

Is polished concrete slippery?

A properly finished polished floor is no more slippery than any other hard-surface flooring; we can adjust the sheen level to reduce slip risk in wet areas.

Can you add color to polished concrete?

Yes — concrete dye can be applied before final polishing for a translucent color effect that still shows the concrete's natural variation.
