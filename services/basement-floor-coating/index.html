Basement & Below-Grade Floor Coating

A moisture-tolerant coating system built for the below-grade rooms, storm shelters and safe rooms found in North Texas homes.

Basement floor coating is a moisture-tolerant epoxy system designed for below-grade concrete, where vapor transmission from surrounding soil is common and standard coatings can fail. Full basements are rare in DFW because our expansive clay soil and high water table make traditional basement excavation difficult — but many North Texas homes do have below-grade storm shelters, safe rooms, or partially sunken garages and utility rooms, and all of them face the same moisture challenge.

Basement Floor Coating cost in DFW: $5–$9/sq ft installed. Pricing depends on square footage and whether moisture mitigation is needed before coating.

Why below-grade concrete needs special handling

Below-grade slabs are in constant contact with damp soil, which pushes moisture vapor up through the concrete even when the surface looks dry. A standard coating applied over that moisture will blister, bubble or delaminate within months. We test every below-grade slab for moisture vapor emission before quoting and select a moisture-tolerant epoxy formulated to bond and cure even with elevated vapor readings.

How we coat a below-grade floor

The process starts with diagnosis, not just prep:

  1. Moisture testing. We test the slab's vapor emission rate to determine whether a standard or moisture-mitigating primer is required.
  2. Diamond grinding. The slab is mechanically ground to open the surface for bonding.
  3. Crack & joint repair. Cracks are filled and, where relevant, monitored for any active water intrusion before coating.
  4. Moisture-tolerant base coat. A specialty epoxy formulated to cure properly against vapor transmission is applied.
  5. Topcoat & sealing. A durable topcoat finishes the floor with mold- and mildew-resistant properties suited to a below-grade environment.

Storm shelters & safe rooms

Many of our below-grade jobs are actually storm shelters and safe rooms rather than full basements, given how uncommon true basements are in this part of Texas. The same moisture-tolerant approach applies — these spaces sit below grade and face the same vapor exposure as a basement floor.

Basement Floor Coating FAQs

Do many DFW homes have basements?

True basements are uncommon in North Texas because of our expansive clay soil and high water table, which make excavation difficult. Storm shelters, safe rooms and below-grade utility spaces are more common and need the same moisture-tolerant coating approach.

Will the coating bubble or peel from moisture?

Not if we test for vapor emission first and use a moisture-tolerant primer and base coat — skipping that test is the most common cause of below-grade coating failures.

How much does basement floor coating cost?

Typically $5–$9 per square foot, depending on square footage and whether moisture mitigation is required.

Is the coating mold- and mildew-resistant?

Yes, the topcoats we use for below-grade spaces are formulated to resist mold and mildew growth in these naturally damper environments.
