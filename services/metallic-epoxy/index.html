Metallic Epoxy Flooring in Dallas–Fort Worth

A high-gloss, marbled metallic finish that turns a garage, showroom or retail floor into a showpiece — installed by hand, no two floors alike.

Metallic epoxy is a decorative flooring system where mineral pigment powders are hand-manipulated into a wet epoxy base to create a 3D, marbled, high-gloss finish that looks like flowing stone or water. Epoxy Magicians installs metallic floors across Dallas–Fort Worth for garages, home bars, showrooms and retail spaces — every floor is one-of-a-kind because the pattern can't be exactly repeated.

Metallic Epoxy cost in DFW: $8–$12/sq ft installed. A typical 2-car garage runs $3,200–$6,000 installed; showroom and retail pricing depends on square footage and design complexity.

Why homeowners and businesses choose metallic epoxy

Metallic epoxy is the most visually dramatic coating we install. Because the color and movement come from how the installer manipulates the wet pigment — using tools, air, and technique to create cells, lacing and depth — the finish reads as three-dimensional even though the floor is perfectly flat and smooth. It's a popular upgrade for garages that double as living space, car-collector garages, home gyms, and any commercial space where the floor itself is part of the design.

How we install a metallic epoxy floor

Metallic requires more installer skill than any other coating we offer, so prep and application technique both matter:

  1. Diamond grinding. The slab is mechanically ground to open the pores and remove any existing coating or contamination — metallic pigment shows every prep flaw, so this step is non-negotiable.
  2. Crack & joint repair. Cracks and pitting are filled and leveled so they don't disrupt the metallic pattern.
  3. Metallic base application. A clear or tinted epoxy base is mixed with metallic pigment and poured, then manipulated in real time with squeegees, rollers and controlled airflow to create movement.
  4. Cell & lacing development. While the epoxy is still workable, we develop the signature cells, veins and lacing patterns that give metallic floors their depth.
  5. Polyaspartic topcoat. A UV-stable clear topcoat seals and protects the finish, adds slip resistance, and brings out the full gloss.

Metallic vs. flake — which should you choose?

Choose metallic if you want a unique, high-gloss showpiece floor and don't mind a higher price point. Choose flake if you want a durable, slip-resistant, more budget-friendly floor with a more uniform look. Many customers pair a metallic accent area (like a garage entry or bar floor) with flake in higher-traffic zones.

Metallic Epoxy FAQs

How much does metallic epoxy cost in DFW?

Metallic epoxy typically costs $8–$12 per square foot installed. A standard 2-car garage runs about $3,200–$6,000 depending on slab condition and design complexity.

Will my metallic floor look like the photos I've seen?

It will be similar in style but never identical — metallic patterns are hand-created in real time and cannot be exactly duplicated. We'll show you color and pigment samples so you know the palette before we start.

Is metallic epoxy slippery?

The polyaspartic topcoat includes a texture additive for slip resistance. It has more sheen than flake, so we recommend an anti-slip additive for pool decks or entries; garages are typically fine as-is.

Can metallic epoxy go over an old coated floor?

Only if the existing coating is fully removed by grinding first. We never coat over an existing epoxy or paint — adhesion failure is the #1 cause of floor failures we get called to fix.
