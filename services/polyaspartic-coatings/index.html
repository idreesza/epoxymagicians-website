The fastest-curing, most UV-stable coating we install — ready to walk on in hours and built to handle Texas sun without yellowing.

Polyaspartic is a fast-curing polyurea coating that can be installed and driven on within 24 hours, and it resists UV fading far better than standard epoxy. We use polyaspartic as both a stand-alone floor system and as the UV-stable topcoat over epoxy base coats on nearly every job we install — it's the single biggest upgrade over big-box DIY epoxy kits.

Polyaspartic Coatings cost in DFW: $6–$10/sq ft installed. As a full system (not just a topcoat), a 2-car garage typically runs $2,400–$5,000 installed.

Why polyaspartic matters in North Texas

Standard epoxy is not UV-stable — left in direct sun (a driveway, a garage with the door open all day, a patio) it will amber and chalk within a couple of Texas summers. Polyaspartic resin doesn't have that weakness, which is why it's the right choice for driveways, sun-exposed patios, and any garage that gets direct light. It also cures fast enough to install in a single visit, and unlike epoxy, it can be applied across a wider temperature range — useful for winter installs in North Texas cold snaps.

How a polyaspartic install works

Prep is identical to our other coating systems; the difference is in the chemistry and schedule:

  1. Diamond grinding & repair. The slab is ground and any cracks or pitting repaired, exactly as with epoxy.
  2. Polyaspartic base coat. Applied at high build for durability and, if desired, flake or color is broadcast into it.
  3. Same-day topcoat. Because polyaspartic cures fast, the clear UV-stable topcoat can often go down the same day as the base coat — something epoxy systems can't do.
  4. Cure & walk-on. You can typically walk on the floor within a few hours and park vehicles around the 24-hour mark.

Where we use polyaspartic

Polyaspartic is our default topcoat on garage, patio and driveway systems, and our primary recommendation as a full system for driveways and pool decks where UV exposure is constant. It's also used in commercial settings that can't afford multi-day cure windows.

Polyaspartic Coatings FAQs

What's the difference between epoxy and polyaspartic?

Epoxy is more affordable and self-levels well for a smooth base coat, but it can yellow in UV light and takes longer to cure. Polyaspartic is UV-stable, cures in hours instead of days, and can be installed in a wider range of temperatures.

How long until I can drive on a polyaspartic floor?

Typically about 24 hours, compared to 3–5 days for full epoxy cure. You can usually walk on it within a few hours of the final coat.

Can polyaspartic be installed outdoors in Texas heat?

Yes — it's actually the preferred choice for outdoor concrete because it resists UV fading, unlike standard epoxy.

Is polyaspartic more expensive than epoxy?

Slightly, per square foot, but the faster install (less labor time) and no-yellowing durability often make it the better value over the life of the floor.
