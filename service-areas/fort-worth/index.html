Neighborhoods we coat in Fort Worth

We regularly install floors across Fort Worth, including Arlington Heights, Mira Vista, Colonial/TCU-area, Near Southside, Chapel Creek, and neighborhoods near the historic Stockyards. Fort Worth's housing stock spans nearly a century, so our crews are equally comfortable prepping a 1940s bungalow slab and a new-build in a west-side subdivision.

Fort Worth's clay soil and ranch-style garages

Tarrant County sits on the same expansive clay soil common across North Texas, and Fort Worth's many ranch-style homes often have larger-than-average garages and detached workshops built for trucks and trailers. Older Arlington Heights and Near Southside slabs frequently show settling cracks and prior patch jobs; we structurally repair these with a polymer fill before grinding rather than coating over them, which is the most common shortcut that causes early coating failure.

Epoxy flooring cost in Fort Worth: flake systems run $5–$8/sq ft, metallic $8–$12/sq ft. A typical 2-car garage is $1,800–$4,500 installed; larger ranch-style garages and shops are quoted after measuring.
