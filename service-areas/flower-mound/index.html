Shops, barns & larger slab footprints

Flower Mound's larger lots often mean a detached shop or barn floor in addition to the attached garage, and these outbuilding slabs are sometimes poured to a lower spec than the main house. We evaluate outbuilding slabs separately at the estimate — older barn or shop floors may need more extensive crack repair or a moisture check before coating, especially on properties near Grapevine Lake where the water table runs higher.
