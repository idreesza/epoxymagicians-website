Why Plano slabs need the right prep

Plano sits on North Texas expansive clay soil that swells in wet weather and shrinks in drought. That seasonal movement is the leading cause of the hairline and control-joint cracks we see in Plano garages — especially in older neighborhoods east of Central Expressway. It's also why a coating is only as durable as its prep. On every Plano job we diamond-grind the slab, fill and reinforce cracks with a structural polymer, and select a moisture-tolerant coating chemistry rather than acid-etching and hoping. That's the difference between a floor that lasts 15–20 years and one that peels the first summer.
