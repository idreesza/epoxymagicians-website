Custom-home slabs & larger garage footprints

Southlake's custom-built homes generally have well-engineered slabs, but the region's clay soil still causes seasonal movement over time, and larger garage and shop floors mean more control joints to manage during grinding and coating. We plan the control-joint layout with you before install — either honoring the joints in the finished pattern or filling them flush, depending on the look you want for a showcase garage.
