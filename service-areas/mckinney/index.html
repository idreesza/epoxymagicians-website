Epoxy Magicians installs garage floor epoxy, metallic epoxy and polyaspartic coatings throughout McKinney, Texas — a city where we regularly work on both century-old slabs near the historic square and brand-new construction in Craig Ranch.

Neighborhoods we coat in McKinney

We regularly install floors across McKinney, including Stonebridge Ranch, Craig Ranch, Historic Downtown McKinney, Eldorado, and Trinity Falls. McKinney's mix of century-old homes near the square and fast-growing new subdivisions to the west means we see a wider range of slab ages here than almost anywhere else in DFW.

Old square vs. new subdivision slabs

Homes near Historic Downtown McKinney often have original slabs that are 60-100+ years old, with settling cracks and prior patch repairs from decades of North Texas clay soil movement — these need more thorough crack chasing and structural polymer repair before coating. Newer Craig Ranch and Trinity Falls slabs are younger but still need full diamond grinding to remove curing compound. Either way, we assess your specific slab at the estimate rather than quoting blind.

Epoxy flooring cost in McKinney: flake garage systems run $5–$8/sq ft, metallic $8–$12/sq ft. A typical 2-car McKinney garage is $1,800–$4,800 installed — older downtown-area slabs often land at the higher end due to additional crack repair.

Popular in McKinney garages

  • Flake floors — the most requested finish for older McKinney homes since the broken color pattern hides settling cracks and patch repairs.
  • Metallic epoxy — popular in Stonebridge Ranch and Craig Ranch's larger, newer garages.
  • Polyaspartic driveway coatings — a frequent add-on for McKinney's tree-lined older streets where driveways see heavy shade-to-sun transitions.
