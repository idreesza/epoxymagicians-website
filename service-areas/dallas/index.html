Epoxy Magicians installs garage floor epoxy, metallic epoxy and commercial coatings throughout Dallas, Texas — a city with more slab variety than almost anywhere else in the metroplex, from century-old East Dallas bungalows to converted warehouse lofts in Deep Ellum.

Neighborhoods we coat in Dallas

We regularly install floors across Dallas, including Lakewood, the M Streets, Preston Hollow, Oak Cliff/Bishop Arts, Uptown, and the converted warehouse and loft spaces of Deep Ellum and the Design District. Because Dallas spans multiple soil zones and building eras, we treat every neighborhood's slabs differently rather than applying a one-size prep approach.

Old East Dallas slabs vs. new construction

Lakewood and M Streets homes often sit on original 1920s-40s slabs with decades of settling cracks and prior patch work, requiring more thorough structural crack repair before coating. Preston Hollow and newer North Dallas construction have younger slabs but still need full diamond grinding to remove curing compound residue. Deep Ellum and Design District commercial conversions frequently involve older industrial slabs that need moisture testing before a coating system is selected.

Epoxy flooring cost in Dallas: flake systems run $5–$8/sq ft, metallic $8–$12/sq ft. A typical 2-car garage is $1,800–$4,800 installed, with older East Dallas slabs sometimes landing at the higher end due to crack repair. Commercial and loft conversions are quoted after a walkthrough.

  • Metallic epoxy — frequently requested in Preston Hollow and Uptown for showcase garages and finished spaces.
  • Commercial epoxy — for Deep Ellum and Design District loft, retail and restaurant conversions.
